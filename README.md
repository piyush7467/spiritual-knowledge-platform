# Spiritual Knowledge & Evidence Platform

A public, bilingual (Hindi & English), mobile-first spiritual knowledge and evidence platform centered on the teachings of **Sant Rampal Ji Maharaj**, original scriptures, published translations, saints, commentators, and traceable primary sources.

## Core Principle

> **Every important published claim, quotation, comparison, and AI answer must be traceable to verified source evidence.**
